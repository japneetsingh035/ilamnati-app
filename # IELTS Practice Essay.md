Universities and colleges could hold educational events to teach students about their political rights. Another solution is to promote youth participation in politics. This can ensure representation for young citizens and amplify their voices in policy decisions. For instance, Nelson Mandela was a young activist who fought for justice and later became South Africa’s first black president, demonstrating the power of youth involvement in politics.

In conclusion, the lack of youth voting participation can delay necessary political change and result in policies that do not serve younger generations. However, education campaigns and promoting youth in politics can help tackle these problems.
